Cookie Policy

This policy explains how littlego.ng, operated by NetPole Solutions Limited, uses cookies and similar technologies, and how you can control them. It forms part of our Privacy Policy.

What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage, work in a comparable way. They can be strictly necessary for a site to work, or used for other purposes such as measuring visits, which need your permission.

When we ask for your choice

The first time you visit, we ask you to choose before you continue. You can choose "Necessary only", "Accept all", or pick categories yourself. Choosing "Necessary only" does not stop you using the website or joining the waitlist.

Advertising and social media cookies: we do not use any.

Third parties: our fonts, images and code are served from littlego.ng itself, so visiting the site does not send your data to font, advertising or social media companies. Our hosting provider records basic technical information, such as IP addresses, for security, as described in our Privacy Policy.

How to change your choice

  • Use Cookie settings at the bottom of the home page at any time.
  • You can also clear or block cookies and site data in your browser settings. If you clear site data, we will ask for your choice again on your next visit.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use cookies or similar technologies, we will update this page and, where needed, ask for your choice again.
