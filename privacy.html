1. Who we are

NetPole Solutions Limited is the data controller for personal data collected through littlego.ng. This means we decide how and why your personal data is processed, and we are responsible for it.

2. Our data processing principles

We process personal data in line with the principles of the NDP Act:

  • Lawfulness, fairness and transparency: we tell you what we collect and why, and we have a lawful basis for it.
  • Purpose limitation: we collect data only for specified, explicit and legitimate purposes.
  • Data minimisation: we collect only what we need.
  • Accuracy: we keep data accurate and up to date, and correct it when you ask.
  • Storage limitation: we keep data only as long as necessary.
  • Integrity and confidentiality: we protect data with appropriate technical and organisational measures.
  • Accountability: we can show how we comply with the NDP Act and GAID.

3. Personal data we collect

When you join the waitlist, you give us:

  • your full name and email address;
  • your phone number, only if you choose to give it;
  • the general area you live in (for example, "Shomolu / Bariga / Palmgrove"), chosen from a list;
  • the types of journeys you're interested in and roughly when you might need them;
  • your consent choice, the wording you agreed to, and when.

When you visit the website, our hosting provider automatically records technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the site and keep it secure. When you submit the waitlist form, we store a scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address, which cannot easily be turned back into the address, to block spam and repeated sign-ups. If you came from one of our campaigns, we also record the campaign name in the link (for example, "instagram").

We do not collect your children's names or details, their schools, your home address, identity numbers (such as NIN or BVN), payment details or any sensitive personal data through this website.

5. Your consent

  • We ask for your consent before adding you to the waitlist. You choose "I agree" or "I do not agree"; if you choose "I do not agree", we do not add you and do not keep the details you typed.
  • Your consent is freely given, specific, informed and unambiguous. Joining the waitlist is optional and is not a condition of anything else.
  • You can withdraw your consent at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any email from us, or by emailing hello@littlego.ng. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing we carried out before you withdrew it.

6. Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us run the website and waitlist: our website and database hosting provider, and our email service provider. They process data only on our instructions and under contracts that require confidentiality and security.
  • Regulators, law enforcement or courts, such as the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC), where the law requires us to.

7. Transfers outside Nigeria

Our website and waitlist database are hosted by Render Services, Inc. on servers in Frankfurt, Germany. Germany is subject to the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation, which provides a high level of protection for personal data. We transfer data outside Nigeria only in the ways the NDP Act and GAID allow, including under contractual data protection terms with our providers, and we will tell you if this changes.

8. How long we keep it

  • Waitlist details: until you withdraw your consent or ask us to delete them. Otherwise, we delete or anonymise them no later than 24 months after LittleGo launches in your area.
  • Records of consent: for as long as we need to show that we had your consent, and no longer than required by law.
  • Technical information: our hosting provider keeps server logs for a limited period for security and troubleshooting.

9. How we protect it

We use technical and organisational measures appropriate to the data we hold, including:

  • encrypted connections (HTTPS) between your browser and our servers;
  • access to waitlist data limited to authorised team members with individual, password-protected accounts;
  • storing IP addresses only in hashed form;
  • spam protection and limits on repeated submissions;
  • regular review of these measures as LittleGo grows.

10. Your rights

Under the NDP Act and GAID, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected;
  • have your data deleted (the "right to be forgotten");
  • restrict how we process your data;
  • receive your data in a commonly used, machine-readable format (data portability);
  • object to processing, including for marketing communications;
  • not be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing;
  • withdraw your consent at any time;
  • complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) at ndpc.gov.ng. We'd appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first.

11. Making a request

To use any of your rights, email hello@littlego.ng with the subject "Data protection request". Tell us which right you want to use and the email address you used to join the waitlist. We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting on the request. Requests are free of charge, and we will respond within the timelines set by the NDP Act and GAID.

12. Children's privacy

This website is for parents and guardians aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through it, and the waitlist does not ask for any information about your children. If we learn that a child has given us personal data, we will delete it.

13. Cookies and technical information

We use only what is strictly necessary to run the website and remember your privacy choice. Anything else, such as analytics, runs only if you allow it. See our Cookie Policy for details. You can change your choice at any time using "Cookie settings" at the bottom of the home page.

14. If a data breach happens

If a personal data breach occurs, we will:

  • act promptly to contain it and reduce the risk;
  • notify the NDPC within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, where the NDP Act requires;
  • inform affected people without undue delay where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to their rights and freedoms, and tell them what they can do to protect themselves.

15. Links to other websites

Our website may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please read their privacy policies.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices, the law or LittleGo's services. The current version will always be on this page. If we make significant changes that affect people on the waitlist, we will also tell them by email.
