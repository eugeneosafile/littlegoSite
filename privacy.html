Waitlist privacy notice

This notice explains what happens to the details you give us when you join the LittleGo early-access waitlist on littlego.ng.

Who we are

LittleGo is a brand of Netpole Solutions. Netpole Solutions is responsible for the details you share through this website.

What we collect

  • Your full name and email address.
  • Your phone number, only if you choose to give it.
  • The general area you live in (for example, "Lekki / Ajah"), chosen from a list.
  • The types of journeys you're interested in and roughly when you might need them.
  • That you agreed to receive launch updates, and when.

We deliberately do not ask for your children's names, their schools, or your home address.

Why we collect it

  • To email you updates about LittleGo's launch and early access.
  • If you gave a phone number, to contact you about early access in your area.
  • To understand where demand is, so we can decide which areas and journeys to start with.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your details.
  • Joining the waitlist is not a booking and doesn't create any agreement for transport services.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from updates at any time using the link in any email we send, or by emailing us. You can also ask us to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you.

Contact

For any question about your details, or to make a request, email hello@littlego.ng.
