Safety and trust

The principles we're building on.

These are the safeguards LittleGo is being designed around. Before any child travels with us, we'll publish exactly what is in place and how it works.

Our plain-language promise. Everything here describes planned safeguards, not services running today. We won't call anything live until it is.

  • Provider vetting and onboarding

    Operators, drivers and the adults travelling with children are intended to pass defined checks and onboarding before they take part.

  • Appropriate supervision

    Journeys designed for children only, with supervision suited to the child and the trip. No unrelated adult passengers.

  • Journey visibility

    Parents should know when a journey starts, how it's going and when it ends, with a clear way to reach a real person.

  • Controlled handover

    A child should only be handed to an adult the parent has approved. If nobody approved is there, the child stays safe until they are.

FAQ

Questions parents ask.

We'll add more as we get closer to launch.

What is LittleGo?

A parent-directed platform being developed to coordinate children's everyday journeys through independent transport operators. We start with school-to-home trips, and plan to add lessons, sports, clubs and visits to family.

Is LittleGo available yet?

Not yet. LittleGo is currently being developed and is not taking bookings. Joining the waitlist is the best way to hear first when early access opens.

Which locations will LittleGo serve first?

We're starting with a pilot in Lagos. Where we go next depends partly on demand, so telling us your general area on the waitlist genuinely helps us decide.

What types of journeys will be supported?

School runs come first. Over time we plan to support lessons and tutoring, sports and clubs, and other trusted destinations such as grandparents' homes.

How will safety be approached?

Through provider vetting and onboarding, appropriate supervision, journey updates for parents, and handover only to adults you approve. These are planned safeguards, and we'll publish exactly what is in place before launch. Read our safety principles.

How can I join the early-access list?

Fill in the short waitlist form. We only ask for your name, email, a general area and the journeys you're interested in. Joining is free, isn't a booking, and doesn't guarantee service availability.

What will you do with my details?

We use them to send you launch updates and to understand where demand is. We don't sell them. You can unsubscribe or ask us to delete them at any time. See the privacy notice.
