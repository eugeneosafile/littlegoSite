About LittleGo

Built around the questions parents actually ask

Who is driving? Who is with my child? Did they get there? Who did they go home with? LittleGo is being designed so those questions have clear answers — through independent transport operators, coordinated on your behalf.

  • Safety by design

    Trust and accountability are being built into the service from the start, not added on later.

  • Trusted operators

    We plan to work only with transport providers who meet defined onboarding requirements.

  • Parent-led

    Journeys organised around your family's needs — you decide where, when and who receives your child.

  • More than school

    School runs first. Over time, lessons, sports, clubs and visits to family too.

Safety and trust

The principles we're building on

Safety isn't a feature we bolt on. These are the safeguards LittleGo is being designed around. We'll share exactly what is in place, and how it works, before any child travels with us.

Plain-language promise: everything on this page describes planned safeguards, not services that are running today. We won't claim a safeguard is live until it is.

  • Provider vetting and onboarding

    Transport operators, drivers and the people travelling with children are intended to pass defined checks and onboarding before they take part.

  • Appropriate supervision

    Journeys are being designed for children only, with supervision arrangements suited to the child and the trip — no unrelated adult passengers.

  • Journey visibility and communication

    Parents should know when a journey starts, how it's going and when it ends, with a clear way to reach a real person.

  • Controlled handover

    A child should only ever be handed to an adult the parent has approved — and if nobody approved is there, the child stays safe until they are.

FAQ

Questions parents ask

Something we haven't answered? We'll be adding more as we get closer to launch.

What is LittleGo?

LittleGo is a parent-directed platform being developed to coordinate children's everyday journeys through independent transport operators. We start with school-to-home trips, and plan to add lessons, sports, clubs and visits to family.

Is LittleGo available yet?

Not yet. LittleGo is currently being developed and is not taking bookings. Joining the waitlist is the best way to hear first when early access opens.

Which locations will LittleGo serve first?

We're starting with a pilot in Lagos. Where we go next depends partly on demand, so telling us your general area on the waitlist genuinely helps us decide.

What types of journeys will be supported?

School runs come first. Over time we plan to support lessons and tutoring, sports and clubs, and other trusted destinations such as grandparents' homes.

How will safety be approached?

Through provider vetting and onboarding, appropriate supervision, journey updates for parents, and handover only to adults you approve. These are planned safeguards — we'll publish exactly what is in place before launch. Read our safety principles.

How can I join the early-access list?

Fill in the short waitlist form. We only ask for your name, email, a general area and the journeys you're interested in. Joining is free, isn't a booking, and doesn't guarantee service availability.

What will you do with my details?

We use them to send you launch updates and to understand where demand is. We don't sell them. You can unsubscribe or ask us to delete them at any time. See the privacy notice.
